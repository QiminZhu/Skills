# Assessment and evidence-based progression

Assessment selects the next teaching move. Align each attempt with the objective's successful-performance criterion and target depth. Use session-only evidence; never infer full understanding, stable mastery or retention from a score or one success.

## Diagnosis and assistance
Show the lesson summary before the initial diagnostic unless the learner explicitly requests otherwise. Use stated knowledge and a brief low-stakes attempt when useful; do not force a test before a requested explanation. Default to one focused question unless a batch is requested. Inspect actual work before diagnosing a prerequisite gap, concept error, procedure slip, strategy choice, representation problem or ambiguous question.

Distinguish independent, prompted, scaffolded and not-yet-demonstrated attempts. Correctness alone does not establish independence: hints, exposed solutions, feedback-assisted retries and worked target steps are assistance. Treat categories as descriptions of attempts, not learner labels. Apply [scaffolded-independence.md](scaffolded-independence.md) for targeted help and fresh unassisted checks.

## Understanding evidence at the target depth
Choose the relevant dimensions before assessing; not every objective requires all five:
- Explanation: state the mechanism/relationship in the learner's own words.
- Application: solve a fresh problem independently.
- Reasoning: explain why the conclusion or method works.
- Discrimination: choose among confusable methods and justify the choice.
- Transfer: apply it in a changed but within-scope context or representation.

Qualifying independent evidence must match the objective's criterion, target depth and relevant dimensions, with no target-skill hints or exposed answer before submission. A fresh task must not merely repeat a just-revealed answer. Recognition cannot verify explanation or transfer. A correct answer with faulty reasoning does not meet a reasoning criterion. Seek a follow-up when an answer is guessed, ambiguous, incomplete or otherwise insufficient; do not impose a fixed question count or demand reasoning for a simple fact where it adds no evidence.

Assess correctness, method, relationships and reasoning, not eloquence. Allow defensible alternatives. Identify what was demonstrated, at what depth and with what support, and what remains unknown. Current independent performance is not proof of durable retention or absence of outside help. Later checking is needed for retention; do not schedule it automatically.

## Difficulty ladder: adapt per objective
| Level | Demand |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Foundations | Identify the concept and explain its basic meaning |
| 2. Familiar application | Apply it to a straightforward familiar example |
| 3. Reasoning | Explain why, diagnose an error, or handle an edge case |
| 4. Strategy selection | Choose between related methods and justify trade-offs |
| 5. Transfer and integration | Apply in an unfamiliar within-scope context or combine taught concepts |

Use only relevant levels. This is not a rigid universal ordering: select tasks that fit the objective, and do not manufacture five stages for simple facts. Target depth follows source demands and the learner's goal, not a mandatory Level 5.

## Select the next task from evidence
- Clear independent success at the current demand supports advancing to the next meaningful depth. Strong initial evidence can skip unnecessary introductory tasks.
- Correct but guessed/ambiguous responses: briefly check the missing evidence before advancing. A local correct/incorrect flag alone cannot establish reasoning or transfer.
- Hinted or feedback-assisted success: offer a fresh unassisted task at comparable difficulty before advancing. If declined, leave independent verification pending; do not block a direct request to continue.
- Wrong answer: diagnose the smallest supported breakdown and target it. One error does not justify resetting all objectives or labeling a stable misconception.
- An isolated calculation/transcription slip with sound reasoning needs a focused correction, not an automatic drop to foundations.
- Mixed evidence: retain roughly comparable demand and vary the task; do not claim a trend.
- Raise reasoning, constraints, strategy choice or transfer, not confusing wording, tedious arithmetic or unnecessary length. Introduce new prerequisites explicitly before requiring them.
- Adapt separately: a learner can have advanced tracing evidence and basic complexity evidence. Preserve both.
- Honor requests for easier/harder questions, a pause, explanation, direct solution or a different format. User-requested challenge is allowed but is not evidence a previous level was passed.

Track objective ID, criterion/target depth, actual demand, outcome, assistance, uncertainty and the reason for the next task in temporary conversation context. Advancing means choosing the next appropriate question, not auto-advancing the host UI; preserve the separate Next action.

## Session close
Summarize actual performance and support, unresolved gaps and a useful next task. Use the lecture completion gates in [lecture-coverage.md](lecture-coverage.md) for whole lectures. Never claim a durable record, scheduled review, cross-plugin mastery update or universal understanding.

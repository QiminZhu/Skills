# Question authoring checklist

For each final draft:
1. Does the question test the intended objective, not just a related term?
2. Is the full prompt answerable with its stated assumptions and without hidden context?
3. Is the source citation real and relevant?
4. Solve without consulting the draft answer key; then inspect every option.
5. Is the complete correct option set unique and consistent with `correct`?
6. Are wrong choices plausible, distinct, and actually wrong?
7. Does every option-specific explanation accurately address its own option?
8. Does the overall explanation accurately explain the principle?
9. Are difficulty and cognitive level justified by the required task?
10. Are the question ID, schema, version, and draft status valid?

This self-check is not a substitute for independent Question Validator review.
